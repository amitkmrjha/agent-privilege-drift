## Decision rule (plan)

The plan: if power is below 80%, move to 5 seeds. With 3 seeds (noise x1): the plain comparison at 0.05/18 has 76.5%; the primary difference-in-differences at 0.05 has 78.7%. At least one is below 80%: move to 5 seeds.

With 5 seeds: 96.5% and 94.2%. If the run-to-run noise is twice the estimate, 5 seeds give 64.9% and 71.1%.

Cost of 5 instead of 3 seeds: 900 more runs per model (67% more time and calls, since both are linear in runs).


This is the power to detect a difference of the designed size, not a forecast of the true effect.
